[B]v2.0.9[/B]
- Added a new [B]Dark Obsidian[/B] theme with a pure black color palette and neutral gray highlights. Suited for OLED displays and night-time viewing.
- Added a comprehensive fallback mechanism to retrieve information from TMDbHelper for PVR items (e.g. TV Recordings, VOD, etc.) with incomplete metadata.
[B]Basic[/B] provides plot, clearlogo and ratings. [B]Detailed[/B] adds studio logos, genres and other information, and attempts to pass TMDb information to the player for a richer OSD.
Go to [B]Skin Settings → Other → PVR TMDbHelper lookups[/B] to learn more.
- Other bug fixes and enhancements.
